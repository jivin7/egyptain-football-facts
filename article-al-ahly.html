Al Ahly: Africa’s Red Devils

Al Ahly Sporting Club are more than a football team. Founded in 1907 in Cairo, the Red Devils became the standard of success in Egyptian and African club football.

A club built for winning

Ahly’s domestic record is unmatched in Egypt. League titles, cups, and Super Cups fill the trophy room. For Ahly fans, challenging for every competition every season is not ambition — it is expectation.

That pressure creates a unique culture. Young players who debut in red instantly feel the weight of history. Experienced stars are judged by titles, not only highlights.

Kings of the CAF Champions League

On the continental stage, Al Ahly are the most successful club in CAF Champions League history. African nights in Cairo — with red flares, drums, and packed stands — are famous across the continent.

Those campaigns also send Ahly to the FIFA Club World Cup, giving Egyptian football rare global club matchdays against European and South American champions.

Derby identity

No Ahly story is complete without Zamalek. The Cairo Derby defines weekly conversation in the capital. Ahly fans measure eras by both continental trophies and derby nights that become family stories.

Quick Ahly facts

  • Founded in 1907 in Cairo.
  • Nickname: The Red Devils.
  • Most successful club in CAF Champions League history.
  • Record Egyptian Premier League winners.
  • Academy and first team have produced countless Egypt internationals.
