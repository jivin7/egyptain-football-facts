Who we are

Egyptian Football Facts is an independent fan website dedicated to Egyptian football culture. We publish original articles, club fact pages, and educational guides about the Pharaohs, the Cairo Derby, the Egyptian Premier League, AFCON history, and World Cup campaigns.

What we offer

  • Original articles about Egyptian football history and culture
  • Search any Egyptian club and open its fact page
  • World Cup Group G standings and Egypt knockout path
  • Curated facts about clubs, players, matches, competitions, and legends
  • Football video reels and saved favourites on your device

Our mission

We want every Egypt fan — at home or abroad — to discover something new about the Pharaohs, Al Ahly, Zamalek, canal-city clubs, and Egypt on the world stage. Our content is written for education and entertainment.

Data sources

Club facts and articles on this site are curated locally for fans. World Cup Group G standings reflect published final group stage results. Content is for information and entertainment and may not always reflect the very latest transfer or injury news.
