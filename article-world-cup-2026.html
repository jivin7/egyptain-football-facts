Egypt at the FIFA World Cup 2026

World Cup summers are special in Egypt. In 2026 the Pharaohs returned to the global stage in Group G, finished second, and earned a knockout path that every Egyptian fan can recite.

Group G final picture

Egypt competed in Group G with Belgium, Iran, and New Zealand. The final group table placed Belgium first and Egypt second, both on five points. Iran finished third and New Zealand fourth.

That second-place finish meant qualification for the Round of 32 — a huge result for Egyptian football and a reward for a resilient group campaign.

What the knockout path looks like

As Group G runners-up, Egypt’s Round of 32 opponent is Australia. Win that tie and the Pharaohs would face Argentina in the Round of 16. It is a demanding route, but also a historic opportunity: Egyptian fans dream of a first deep World Cup knockout run.

Why this tournament matters

Egypt’s World Cup history includes early appearances and long waits between finals. Every modern campaign is measured against AFCON glory and the hope of rewriting World Cup knockout history. 2026 gives a new generation of fans their own Pharaohs tournament memories.

World Cup 2026 facts
