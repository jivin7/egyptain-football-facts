Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Egyptian Football Facts (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit egyptainfootballfacts.com.

Who we are

Egyptian Football Facts is an informational website about Egyptian football clubs, players, matches, competitions, and football culture. Contact us via our Contact Us page.

Information we collect

We do not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or payment details unless you choose to email us through the contact form (which opens your own email app).

Like most websites, standard technical data may be processed by hosting providers and advertising partners (for example IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited) to operate the site and serve ads.

Data stored on your device

We use browser localStorage to keep preferences on your device only, such as:

  • Saved facts and saved videos
  • Video likes and comments you add on this site

This information stays in your browser. Clearing site data removes it. We do not receive this localStorage data on our own servers.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Please note:

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

You can also read how Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by Google and other advertising partners to:

  • Show ads
  • Limit how many times you see an ad
  • Measure ad performance
  • Personalize ads based on your interests (if you have not opted out)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how ads or site features work.

Third-party services

Our site may load content or scripts from third parties such as Google Fonts, Google AdSense, and video hosts. Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

This site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

Your choices

  • Clear localStorage and cookies in your browser
  • Opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings
  • Contact us with privacy questions

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date below shows the latest version.
