Club profile

Zamalek: The White Knights

Zamalek Sporting Club, founded in 1911, are Cairo’s White Knights — a club defined by style, loyalty, and an eternal rivalry with Al Ahly.

White shirts, huge pride

Zamalek’s all-white kit is one of the most recognisable looks in African football. Fans call the club the school of art when teams play with flair. Skillful attackers and creative midfielders are part of Zamalek’s self-image.

Support is intense. From Cairo neighbourhoods to fans abroad, White Knights supporters treat derby week like a national holiday and cup runs like festivals.

Trophies and continental nights

Zamalek have won Egyptian league titles and cups across multiple eras. Continental success, including CAF competitions and Club World Cup appearances after African triumphs, gave the club nights that still live in highlight reels.

The 2019 CAF Confederation Cup win is a modern reminder that Zamalek can still deliver silverware on the biggest African stages.

Life in the Cairo Derby

For Zamalek fans, beating Ahly is priceless. Derby goals create legends. Derby defeats fuel the next meeting. The rivalry is older than many football associations and remains the heartbeat of Egyptian club culture.

Quick Zamalek facts

  • Founded in 1911.
  • Nickname: The White Knights.
  • Share Cairo with rivals Al Ahly.
  • Known for passionate ultras and choreographed displays.
  • Produced stars and cult heroes such as Shikabala.
